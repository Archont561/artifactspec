## Context

Agents and authors need actionable feedback while authoring and need to understand what a renderer produced and where it came from.

## Decision

Plan shared validation and an LSP with source-linked diagnostics, plus explainable rendering and explicit fidelity or fallback reporting across artifact outputs.

## Consequences

Reference, type, phrase and capability diagnostics can prevent unsupported requests but cannot guarantee factual or visual correctness. Interfaces, scope and implementation remain to be designed; no LSP or general validator exists yet.
